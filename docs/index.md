# FracLap

FracLap is a Lean 4 and Mathlib library for the fractional Laplacian on the line and the half-line. It covers stable heat kernels, semigroups, Dirichlet forms, maximum principles, and functional inequalities.

The [Knowledge section](knowledge/index.md) presents each mathematical statement alongside its Lean declarations and LaTeX source. The [interactive blueprint](blueprint/index.html) shows dependencies between results.

- [Browse the mathematics](knowledge/index.md)
- [Read the bibliography](knowledge/bibliography.md)
- [Build and verify the library](setup.md)
- [Learn about the development workflow](workflow.md)

The development began with Bartosz Wróblewski’s 2016 master’s thesis. Individual chapters state the assumptions and scope of their results.
